# Source Evaluation

## Goal

Assess whether a source is appropriate for a specific claim.

## Questions

- Who published it?
- When?
- What population or dataset does it cover?
- What methodology was used?
- Is the source primary, secondary, or tertiary?
- What claim does it actually support?
- What does it not establish?
- Are there conflicts of interest or important limitations?

## Rule

Source quality is contextual. Do not assign a universal score without a defined evaluation framework.
