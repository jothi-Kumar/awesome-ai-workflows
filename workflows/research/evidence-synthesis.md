# Evidence Synthesis

## Goal

Combine multiple sources without hiding disagreement or uncertainty.

## Instructions

1. Extract the relevant claim from each source.
2. Record source date and population/scope.
3. Assess whether sources address the same question.
4. Identify agreement.
5. Identify disagreement.
6. Distinguish source findings from your synthesis.
7. State important limitations.
8. Avoid false precision.
