# Requirements Clarification

## Goal

Turn ambiguous requests into testable requirements.

## Instructions

1. Extract the stated objective.
2. Identify actors/users.
3. Identify inputs and outputs.
4. Identify constraints.
5. Identify acceptance criteria.
6. Identify dependencies.
7. List unresolved questions.
8. Avoid inventing requirements.

## Expected output

```text
Objective:
Users:
Inputs:
Outputs:
Constraints:
Acceptance criteria:
Dependencies:
Open questions:
```
