# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, or processes rather than people.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and intimidation.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Avoid deliberately misleading claims about AI capabilities or project behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include:

- Harassment or threats
- Personal attacks
- Discriminatory comments
- Publishing private information without permission
- Deliberate disruption
- Malicious content or unsafe instructions
- Repeated bad-faith behavior

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or restrict participation when behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

Reports should be made privately through the contact method documented in `SECURITY.md` or the repository's maintainer contact.

We will handle reports as confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and project-related interactions where participants are representing or participating in the project.
